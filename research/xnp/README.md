# XERSXES Node Protocol (XNP) research

A simulation-first effort for authenticated, authorized exchange of non-safety-critical telemetry and environmental observations among compatible nodes.

Phase 1 is a logical graph of synthetic drone nodes over ordinary IP networking. No physical radio mesh or real aircraft control is part of the MVP.

Future experiments may reuse MAVLink/MAVSDK, established cryptographic transports, and manufacturer-supported drone integration. Flight control and emergency safety must remain independent of XNP.
